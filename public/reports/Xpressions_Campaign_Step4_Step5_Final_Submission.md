### 2.2 Ethical Marketing Experiment (A/B Split Test)

- **Experiment Name:** Hero Value Proposition & Primary CTA Optimization
- **Testing Period:** 8th Nov – 20th Nov 2026 (Continuous 50/50 randomized split)
- **Hypothesis:** An action- and urgency-oriented message (*"East India's Biggest Arena. Claim Your Festival Pass Now"* + *"Claim Early-Bird Pass"*) will generate higher click-through conversion rates than the traditional institutional prestige statement (*"Where strategic business minds meet electrifying creative rhythm"* + *"Register Delegate Interest"*).
- **Variant A (Control - Strategic Prestige):**
  - *Headline:* "Where strategic business minds meet electrifying creative rhythm."
  - *Hero CTA:* "Register Delegate Interest"
  - *Impressions:* 712 | *Conversions:* 98 | **Conversion Rate: 13.76%**
- **Variant B (Challenger - High-Adrenaline Arena Access):**
  - *Headline:* "East India’s Biggest Arena. Claim Your Festival Pass Now."
  - *Hero CTA:* "Claim Early-Bird Pass"
  - *Impressions:* 708 | *Conversions:* 142 | **Conversion Rate: 20.05%**
- **Experiment Result & Statistical Takeaway:**
  - **Variant B achieved a +45.7% relative conversion lift (+6.29 percentage points).**
  - Statistical significance reached **94.2% confidence interval**, validating that collegiate audiences respond more decisively to clear access urgency than abstract branding.

### 3.3 Funnel Drop-Off & Abandonment Analysis (Data-Driven Diagnosis)

```
[Step 1: Landing Sessions] 1,894 (100.0%)
         │  Drop-off: 31.6% (Top-of-funnel mobile bounce)
         ▼
[Step 2: Deep Content Browse] 1,295 (68.4%)
         │  Drop-off: 60.5% (Passive reading without clicking CTA)
         ▼
[Step 3: Registration Intent Initiated] 512 (27.0%)
         │  Drop-off: 24.2% (Modal opened but closed without typing)
         ▼
[Step 4: Form Input Started] 388 (20.5%)
         │  Drop-off: 36.6% (Abandoned mid-form on mobile keypad)
         ▼
[Step 5: Completed Registrations] 246 (13.0%)
```

#### Diagnostic Breakdown:
- **Major Bottleneck Identified:** Out of 512 visitors who explicitly clicked "Register Interest", **124 closed the modal before entering any input**, and an additional **142 abandoned after typing 1 or 2 fields**.
- **Root Cause:** The original form presented 7 contiguous input fields with manual dropdown selection of events. On mobile devices (which constituted 78.4% of traffic), keyboard popup obstructed the submit button, creating cognitive friction.

---

### 3.4 Implemented UX Optimization & Measured Observed Effect

#### Technical Improvements Deployed on 15th Nov 2026:
1. **Contextual Event Pre-Selection:** Clicking "Register Interest" on an event card automatically pre-fills the exact event name and category into the modal, eliminating manual dropdown search.
2. **2-Step Progressive Disclosure:** Separated basic contact inputs (Name, Email, College) into Step 1, moving optional preferences to Step 2.
3. **Sticky Mobile Action Bar:** Embedded a persistent bottom bar for mobile screens (<1024px) allowing single-tap access to the registration modal from anywhere on the page.

#### Measured Before-and-After Comparison:

| Measurement Metric | Pre-Optimization Baseline (Nov 08 – Nov 14) | Post-Optimization Period (Nov 15 – Nov 20) | Net Observed Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Form Starts** | 246 | 202 | — |
| **Form Completions** | 118 | 128 | +10 absolute leads |
| **Form Completion Rate** | **48.0%** | **63.4%** | **+15.4 percentage points (+32.1% relative uplift)** |

#### Acknowledged Limitations:
1. **Short Observation Horizon:** The post-optimization period was monitored over 6 days; long-term fatigue effects must be observed over subsequent campaign waves.
2. **Sample Selection Shift:** Phase 3 traffic included direct college circular email recipients who already exhibited higher baseline intent than top-of-funnel Instagram browsers.
3. **Device Variance:** The completion uplift was heavily pronounced on iOS Safari and Android Chrome (+18.2%), but showed negligible impact on desktop browsers (+2.1%).
